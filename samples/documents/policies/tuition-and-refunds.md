# Tuition and Refund Policy

## Payment deadlines

Tuition for each term is due on the first day of classes. A late payment fee of $75 is charged if tuition
is not paid within 14 days of the due date. Payment plans with up to four instalments are available on request
from the Student Finance Office.

## Refunds after withdrawal

Refunds depend on when a course is dropped:

- Before the add/drop deadline: 100% refund of tuition for that course.
- Weeks 3-4 of the term: 50% refund.
- Week 5 onwards: no refund.

Refunds are paid to the original payment method within 10 business days. Students who receive financial aid
should contact the Financial Aid Office before withdrawing, as a withdrawal can change their aid eligibility.

## Scholarships

Merit scholarships require a GPA of 3.5 or higher and full-time enrolment (at least 12 credits per term).
Scholarships are reviewed at the end of every academic year.
