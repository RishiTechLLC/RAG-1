# Student Handbook 2026

This handbook describes the academic policies that apply to all enrolled students.

## Attendance

Students are expected to attend every scheduled class. Missing more than 20% of the sessions of a course
without an approved absence may result in a failing grade for that course. Absences for illness require a
medical note submitted through the student portal within five working days.

## Assignments

### Late submission policy

Assignments submitted after the deadline lose 10% of the available marks per calendar day, up to a maximum
of five days. After five days the assignment receives a mark of zero. Extensions must be requested from the
instructor before the deadline and are granted for documented illness, bereavement or approved university
activities.

### Resubmissions

A failed assignment may be resubmitted once, within two weeks of the grade being released. The maximum mark
for a resubmission is capped at a pass (C).

## Grading scale

| Letter | Grade points | Percentage |
|--------|--------------|------------|
| A      | 4.0          | 93-100     |
| A-     | 3.7          | 90-92      |
| B+     | 3.3          | 87-89      |
| B      | 3.0          | 83-86      |
| B-     | 2.7          | 80-82      |
| C+     | 2.3          | 77-79      |
| C      | 2.0          | 70-76      |
| F      | 0.0          | below 70   |

Students must keep a cumulative GPA of at least 2.0 to remain in good academic standing. A GPA below 2.0 for
two consecutive terms results in academic probation.

## Academic integrity

Plagiarism, contract cheating and unauthorised use of generative AI tools are serious offences. A first
offence results in a zero for the assessment and a mandatory integrity workshop. A second offence is referred
to the Academic Conduct Board and may lead to suspension.

## Leave of absence

Students may request a leave of absence of up to two consecutive terms. Requests are submitted to the
Registrar at least four weeks before the start of the term. Students on leave keep their student number and
email account but cannot enrol in courses.
