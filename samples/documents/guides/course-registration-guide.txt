Course Registration Guide

Registration for the next term opens on the date published in the academic calendar. Students register through
the student portal using their student number and university email.

Credit limits. Full-time students take between 12 and 18 credits per term. Taking more than 18 credits requires
approval from your academic advisor and a GPA of at least 3.3.

Prerequisites. The portal blocks registration for courses whose prerequisites are not met. For example, CS201
Data Structures and Algorithms requires CS101, and DS330 Retrieval-Augmented Generation requires DS210 Machine
Learning Foundations. Instructors can grant a prerequisite override in exceptional cases.

Waitlists. When a course is full you can join its waitlist. Seats are offered in waitlist order and must be
accepted within 48 hours, otherwise the seat passes to the next student.

Add/drop. Courses can be added or dropped without penalty until the add/drop deadline, which is usually the end
of the second week of classes. After that date a dropped course appears on the transcript as W (withdrawn).
