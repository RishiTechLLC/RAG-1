# IT Services FAQ

## How do I reset my password?

Go to https://passwordreset.university.edu and verify your identity with the authenticator app registered
to your account. Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are valid for 365 days.

## Is there campus Wi-Fi?

Yes. Connect to the "Campus-Secure" network with your university email and password. The "Campus-Guest"
network is available to visitors for up to 24 hours.

## What software is available to students?

All students get Microsoft 365 (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Teams) and 5 TB of OneDrive storage. Computer
Science and Data Science students also get Azure for Students credits and GitHub Copilot through the
GitHub Student Developer Pack.

## Where can I get help?

The IT Service Desk is in the library, ground floor, open Monday to Friday 8:00-18:00. You can also open a
ticket at https://help.university.edu or call extension 4357 (HELP).
